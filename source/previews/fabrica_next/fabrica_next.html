My recommendations
  1. Checks on every version by default for your apps, so nothing joins or ships unchecked.
  2. Your ways of working, measured: the rules that can be measured in a running app are measured; the others (such as "refined and understated") stay for you and your model to judge, and the check says which.
  3. Offline and in-house: a check that lists anything an app would fetch from the internet, so your apps stay offline.
  4. A rule before joining: checks passed, and an approval, with no request for changes open.
  5. Release notes kept with each release, drafted from what changed, and inside each package.
  6. Each app's own terms, from Ingenium's, inside each package, with the choice to ask on first open.
  7. Later: a copy of every app to your 2 TB drive each week; the checks over time in Insights; Fabrica's issues opened from Troubleshoot.
