# The live preview of three pages' heads (Ingenium 6.93, held)

`python3 build.py OUT.html` writes one page: Build, Focus and Lector in miniature, one at a time (`#build`, `#focus`, `#lector`),
each with its new head running the modules that would ship (`../../build/modules/r284.js`, `r287.js`, `r289.js`), over a small
stand-in for the page beneath (`page.html`). The keys and colours in `page.html` were measured from the built page of 6.92
(computed styles of the head's keys by day and by night, under the pointer and pressed). `sample.html` is Build's own sample page,
taken from the built page.

Published privately for the owner on 12 November 2026: https://claude.ai/artifact/D4tLB2LZEmpEp31RdmBKEh (three heads) and
https://claude.ai/artifact/MoQYvFf97LR8qHs2EZS8NY (Build alone, its first prism).

The owner's verdict on it (12 November 2026): "All of these animations are wrong and stiff and rigid. They feel off and smooth and
natural. The light through the prism is genuinely horrible. The engine button for the Lector page is stiff and not animated and
clickable and the sway is off and rigid. I disliked them all." So every head stays held (`../../build/held.json`).

The owner's verdict on the second live preview (12 November 2026): all its movement still "OFF"; Lector's seal key "does not match the
rest of the Documents designs look or texture"; Focus's mark "does not move with the ball and everything just moves at a weird rythm and
angle"; the prism light "the worst thing that I have seen yet". Then (13 November 2026): "I want you to do as I asked, that's it. I will
not drop anything! I like the seal as wax seal pressed for Lector, implement that idea."

The third live preview (13 November 2026, the same link): Focus's mark lies on the turning ball (in the rings' own frame, on an axle,
one period of 24 seconds for the whole instrument); Lector's seal is the seal key in violet sealing wax on the foot of the front sheet,
lit from height maps and lit again as its parts turn, the ribbon's tails under it, the fan on one period of 12 seconds; Build's prism is
one ray-traced picture on the graphics chip (r289.js has how). All three stay held until the owner's verdict.
