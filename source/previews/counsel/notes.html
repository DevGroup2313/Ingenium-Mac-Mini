What changedYour mentor's seven steps become Your counsellor's five stages, in the room the mentor had

Measured on 6.89, the mentor asked for a press at every turn: three Add keys, eight boxes to type in, Keep or Drop one entry at a time, Find them, Show me paths. The same findings then lived again in Your profile and Your edge. The counsellor is one path: each stage one card, with its place on the rail, a Next key, and nothing to decide that the app can decide. Today, Applications, People, Your profile, Your edge, Goals and plan and Learn stay as they are; the counsellor fills them. A new profile opens on it; Today names its next thing.

1 · What you haveEverything at once, sorted

One key, or a drop: a CV, a LinkedIn export (.zip or .csv), letters, a portfolio, a statement, a profile file from another device, all in one go. The card says what is kept. You never type your profile from scratch. "I would rather type them" is still there.

2 · Your recordRead once, kept at once, marked found

The model reads your sources once and every entry and skill it finds is kept at once, each marked found with where it came from. Looks right makes one yours; Looks right, all does a section; Change and Remove as before. Nothing enters without a source, and nothing is lost to a Drop you did not mean.

3 · What you wantTap, do not type

The eight questions of the talk, one at a time, answered by chips (your own words optional, saved as you type). Then your two aims as cards: a fully funded doctorate (where, the degree, the field, when) and work that fits around it (what, and its shape). Finishing fills What you are looking for and makes the aims goals.

4 · Where you could goOne run, in view

After the talk the counsellor runs by itself (Stop stops it): how to present yourself as a professional, as an academic and the bridge; what transfers; blind spots; what sets you apart; two to four paths with a first step; and dated steps for each aim, from your record. A point about the world is marked as yours to check.

5 · This weekThree things at a time

Built from your aims, the paths, your blind spots and your documents: three in view, the rest folded, each with why and its minutes. Do it for me runs what the model can (looking at your CV with you, reading an app's skills into your record); Go there opens the place. Done ticks the goal's own step. A low-energy day shows one.

For you to sayFour choices I made, each easy to change

  • The counsellor sits second, after Today, where the mentor was, and a new profile opens on it. Would you rather it came first?
  • After you finish the talk, stage 4 starts by itself. Would you rather press a key?
  • Entries found are kept at once and marked found. Would you rather see each one first (the old way), or keep the plain facts at once and be asked only about skills?
  • The chips' words are mine, from your own description of what you want. Send me yours and they replace them.
