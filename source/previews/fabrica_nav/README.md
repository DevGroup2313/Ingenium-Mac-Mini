# Fabrica, easier to find your way: a live preview, waiting for her word

Her request (in her list, kept in the handoffs): Fabrica with full parity with GitHub and beyond it, "easier to navigate than GitHub and
teaching me as I go". Published (private): https://claude.ai/artifact/NdgRNGS3yvtan8RH3rmBSE (version 1). Not built into Ingenium yet.

Fabrica as it is (the four steps over three panels: Your apps; the app with its rooms Now, Story, Plans, Runs, Care and their parts;
Test and ship), with:
- Where you are: a path above the panels (Fabrica › Tide Clock › Plans › Issues) and what GitHub calls the part you are in;
- Go to anything (/ or Ctrl K): rooms and parts, files, issues, pull requests, runs, versions, and things to do;
- The map (M): the whole of Fabrica, each part with a plain line and GitHub's name, where you are marked, the parts learned ticked;
- Counts on the rooms for what waits there (a change not kept, open issues, a failed run, a check to look at), with GitHub's name on hover;
- Teach me as I go (T): the next step read from the app's state (keep, test, look at a failed run, ship); a short lesson over each part
  (what it is, what GitHub calls it, Show me where, Got it); the parts learned counted in the head; keys G then N, S, P, R, C for the rooms.
The app, its versions, plans and runs in the preview are examples.

Version 3 (her words, 17 November 2026: "I have said this MANY TIMES, no new section, page or tab inside of Ingenium should be flat, stiff
and unresponsive when I hover over it ... The "Your apps" entire panel is flat with no animation, and so many more buttons and cards, I
HATE THAT."): every panel, card, row, chip, check and key is raised, lifts under the pointer (the app cards lift and grow, their mark
turns), sinks when pressed, and rises in when its view opens; the panels, the head, the path and the next step lean toward the pointer.
Minimal or reduced motion keeps them still. When built, Fabrica's own panels get the same.
