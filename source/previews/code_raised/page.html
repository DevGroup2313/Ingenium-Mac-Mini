What changes

Raised as the drop zone: each card takes the drop zone's dashed lilac edge, its lilac light falling from the top and its rounder corners, raised at rest as the drop zone is under your pointer; under your pointer it lifts two points with the drop zone's deeper shadow and spring, and it sinks when pressed. By night, the same in the drop zone's night colours.

Each card keeps its words, their type and their order. Nothing else in Code or Fabrica changes. Minimal motion keeps them still.

The cards here do nothing when pressed: they are drawn, not run.
