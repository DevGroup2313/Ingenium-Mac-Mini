What changes from 7.29

  • The key is a small round key in the head's top-right corner, in the welcome door's form: a sunken well holding one key, its words in its tooltip. Pressed, it lights violet while the panel is open.
  • The panel opens beside the diamond: the head's words step aside, the diamond glides toward the middle, and the panel unfolds from the key down the head's right side. On a narrow screen it opens under the diamond.
  • The stones stand alone: each choice is a cut diamond that turns slowly and catches the light, with its name beneath it and no button behind it. The chosen one floats a little higher over a soft light, its name in Ingenium's violet with a dot beneath.
  • The holder's band is two small holders, Ingenium's violet and your own colour, each with its name beneath: no long buttons.
  • The light has Ingenium's own switch: a sunken well when off, a violet key when on, its knob springing across.
  • No scroll bar in view, in the panel or in this preview, as your rule for all your apps says; everything still scrolls.
  • The panel is a slab of frosted glass in a silver bezel like the holder's, its parts engraved, the chosen stone shown in a small silver setting.
  • Unchanged: what each choice does, the same panel in Settings, Everyday, and the diamond with nothing chosen.
