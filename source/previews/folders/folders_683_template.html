Your apps

Your apps

An index card for each of your apps. Each logo sways, leans toward your pointer and knocks when pressed; press it, or anywhere on its card, to open its folder.

Your build library

How each of your apps was built, filed by app and by part, kept on this device. Bring in your build library (the zip of notes) and each app's notes appear in its folder, under How it was built.

Bring in your work

Each is read on this device and nothing is sent anywhere. What comes in is sorted into your folders by the words that name your apps, and you can move any of it.

How I work, on every app

In your own words: how you like to work, what you expect of a model, what you never want. It goes with every conversation in a folder, before the folder's own notes.

A preview of three new things on Folders, in its own design, for you to try before anything changes in Ingenium. The drawer: its left pull finds a folder as you type, and its right pull puts the folders in order (used last, A to Z, Z to A, newest, oldest); the label says how many match. Your apps: each app's seal sways, leans toward your pointer, lifts under it and knocks when pressed, then opens its folder. Any app's logo: open a folder and give it a picture or the app's own page, or drop either on its cover; Harbour Garden here is an invented app wearing its own logo. The folders, notes and conversations are made up. The top bar, the side list and the side buttons are pictures of the app.
