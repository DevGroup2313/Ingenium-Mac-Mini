The learning workbench

Runs in this page; nothing is sent
Make a small model learn, here in this page, on any device, and watch it. These are lessons in how models learn, not rivals to the models you chat with: they are far smaller, and they learn manner, not knowledge. Each tool runs one lesson at a time, can be paused or stopped, and keeps nothing unless you keep it. The graphics chip is used when there is one.

Teach a tiny model to write in the manner of a text of yours, letter by letter, and watch it learn. It learns your text's habits: spellings, punctuation, short phrases.

1. Your text

The model learns only from this text: its letters, spellings, punctuation and rhythm. A few pages is a start; a whole book is better.

At least 500 characters; 20,000 or more teaches more.
