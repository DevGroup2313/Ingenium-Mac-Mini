Models: the turret, made as the compass is

  • Drawn on the graphics chip as a real instrument, face on as the compass is: a rope-milled bezel round a polished case, an engraved memory scale, and a turret plate in guilloche that turns on a spring to bring a cartridge under the violet index, into the engine.
  • Five lens cartridges stand out of the plate, each a milled metal barrel with coated glass whose rings turn slowly; the one in the engine glows softly. Each part casts its own soft shadow.
  • Two finishes to choose between: silver and charcoal (silver metal, a charcoal plate) or purple metal (today's violet, in the same detail). Home's own engine button stands at the heart, with Home's flourishes and click.
IngeniumBlend · the movement of your models

Blend

Ask two or more of your models the same question and bring their answers together. They answer one after another on this device; each answer appears on the right as it is written, with its thinking, and the blended answer comes last.

Make a blend in four steps: choose the models, choose how they work together, ask, then read the answers.

Point at the movement and it leans toward you; press it and it winds.

Blend: the movement, made as the compass is

  • A watchmaker's movement in the compass's metal: three gears in mesh, cut with spokes and turned rings, under a silver bridge with violet screws and diamond jewels in their bearings, on a turned plate inside the same rope-milled case.
  • It runs at its own slow pace, leans toward the hand, and winds when pressed (faster for a moment, then settling). Home's engine button sits at the heart of the great wheel.
  • The same two finishes as Models, so the two pages can match.
