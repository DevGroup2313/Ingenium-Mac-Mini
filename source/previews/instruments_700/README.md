# Models' turret and Blend's movement in the compass's metal (live preview, 13 November 2026, first look)

`mb.html` is the page published privately for the owner (https://claude.ai/artifact/NBu3yGanc5vYsRT31GMbcA, version 1): one renderer on the
graphics chip (`instr.js`, distance fields, the diamond's studio light) drawing the turret (rope-milled bezel, engraved scale, turret plate in
guilloche with a violet enamel ring, five lens cartridges) and the movement (three gears in mesh, a bridge, violet screws, diamond jewels), in
two finishes (silver and charcoal, purple metal), with Home's engine button at the heart. `page_make.py` and `make.py` build it from
`body.html`; their paths point at the scratch kit they were written in. Nothing here is in any build.
