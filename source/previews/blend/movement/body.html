Choose two or more models, how they work together and the effort; then write the question and press Run blend. The models on this device answer one after another; your connected models answer at the same time.

A preview of Blend as a movement in 3D, for you to try before anything changes in Ingenium. Each model you choose is a metal wheel in its own violet. The wheels are rendered with rounded, polished edges and holes you can see through, and a light from the top left stays still while they turn. Ingenium's seal sits in a polished setting at the centre of Ingenium's wheel, and its star turns as it does on Home. The movement faces you and sways a little; when you point at a wheel, it comes to rest facing you. Press a wheel to see it in step I; press the seal to wind the movement. No gold and no glow. The answers here are made up for the preview; the top bar, the side list and the side buttons are pictures of the app.
