Tide Clock on your iPhone and iPad

Four ways. The first two need nothing from Apple. The last two make an app of its own, with its own icon, signed with an Apple ID.

Send to my devicesPaired on Wi-Fi

Your devices paired with this computer, as for lending models. The app goes to Ingenium there and stays, offline too.

iPhoneReady
iPadAsleep
Or as a fileAnywhere

One file with the whole app. Send it by AirDrop, Messages or Files, then open it with Ingenium: Your apps, Add from a file.

From my MacIn-house, the default

Ingenium on your Mac serves it on your home Wi-Fi.

http://mac-mini.local:8765/tide-clock/
  1. On the iPhone, on the same Wi-Fi, open the address in Safari (or point the camera at the code).
  2. Press Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. It opens from its icon while your Mac is on and the iPhone is at home.

Away from home, or with the Mac asleep, it does not open: an iPhone keeps an app offline only from a secure (https) address.

On GitHub PagesThe backup

A secure address, so it works offline anywhere once added. On a free GitHub plan the app's files are public.

The IPA, made hereTide Clock-1.0.ipa
  1. On your Mac, open Sideloadly and join the iPhone by cable (the first time).
  2. Drop the IPA on Sideloadly, and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. On the iPhone, once: Settings, General, VPN and Device Management, trust your Apple ID; then Settings, Privacy and Security, Developer Mode.
