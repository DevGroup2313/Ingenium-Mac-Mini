Ingenium 7.29 · a preview for your word

The diamond's colours, within reach

"I want a clear way to edit Adamas' diamond and change the colours as I please, I can't locate the settings for it. Please make it accessible and visible for me."

What I found: there was no setting to find. Since 7.01 the diamond has changed colour by itself through the day (the clear crystal from 5:00, lavender from 11:00, purple from 14:00, the night stone from 18:00, and the night stone whenever the night theme is on). The code could switch looks, but nothing on screen called it. Every picture below is taken from Ingenium itself, built from the new sources.

Where you find it

A key on Adamas's head, Diamond colours, beside Run your page and Load a sample. It sits on a second row because the three keys are a little wider than the words' column.
The same choices in Settings, Everyday, as a raised tile named "The diamond on Adamas", whose line says what is chosen. Find a setting finds it (try "diamond" or "colour"), and Show me on Adamas opens the head's panel.

The panel, beside the diamond

The panel opens over the head's words, so the diamond stays in full view and changes the moment you choose. The stone: By the hour (as now), Clear crystal, Lavender, Purple, Night stone, or Your own colour. The holder's band: Ingenium's violet (as now) or your own colour. The light that shoots through it, on or off. Back to Ingenium's own undoes everything. Escape or × closes it. On a phone it opens under the keys instead.

A colour of your own

Eight jewel colours (Ruby, Rose, Amber, Citrine, Emerald, Aqua, Sapphire, Amethyst), or any colour from your system's colour picker, with a slider from Faint to Deep. The stone filters light through your colour, as a real coloured gem does. These four are test choices I made to check it.

testRose, by night, lit as the night stone is. Band: Amber. This is the picture with the gold strip you saw: the band was set to Amber to test that option.

With nothing chosen, nothing changes

You asked me to put the strip around the holder back. It was never changed: the gold strip came from my test choice above. To prove it, here is the diamond with nothing chosen, held still at one instant, from 7.28's sources and from the new ones.

Compared pixel by pixel (220,480 pixels each): no pixel differs, by night or by day. The violet band is exactly as it was.

What else this release holds

  • Adamas's chosen tab (Your page, Download as an app) is a solid violet key with white words again, as in Settings, by day and by night.
  • Your apps' packages: I made every kit and every ready-made app of Carpe Diem, Inkwell and Lakay and read each one. Their icons, names and pages were their own, but six small hidden files still said "Ingenium Fabrica" (the Android app's signature, and the file each Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows app keeps about itself). They now say "Fabrica". 137 checks pass; The Forge's 147 checks passed already.
  • Open Storage from the "storage is full" notice now reaches the Storage section with its heading ready.
