1. Who this notice is from

Ingenium is made by Yannie D. Forest. Questions about this notice: the contact its maker adds.

2. What Ingenium keeps, and where

Your work stays on the device you use Ingenium on: chats, notes, memory, settings, projects, apps made in Fabrica and their versions, documents, your career profile, signing keys, your sign-ins to services you link, and the keys you give it for online models. In a browser, in its own storage for Ingenium's page; in the computer apps, also in files in Ingenium's own folder; on a phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. None of it is sent to its maker.

3. Sealed with your password

With protection on (Settings, Security), what Ingenium keeps is sealed with a key made from your password (AES-GCM, 256-bit), and backups are sealed whole. You also receive ten recovery words. If every way back in is lost, no one can open what is sealed. Settings, Security shows each protection's true state.

4. When Ingenium goes online

Only when you use one of these, and only for that use:

  • Downloading a model: Hugging Face.
  • An online model, with your own key: the provider you choose (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or Moonshot AI).
  • Fabrica's link to GitHub: GitHub.
  • Research and search tools: the services you ask, such as Crossref, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, arXiv, Open Library, Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo or Brave Search.
  • Vocatio, a job posting by its link: the posting's own site.
  • Dictation, on a copy without its speech files: those files, fetched once.

Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Settings, Security, Offline only holds back every request to the internet until you allow it.

5. Between your own devices

Your paired devices talk over your home network. Each device that pairs agrees on a key of its own with your computer, and you confirm four words on both screens; then everything between them is sealed end to end. A device paired before, or one that cannot seal, is guarded by the pairing code alone until it pairs again, and Settings shows which.

6. Your choices

Its maker holds no data about you from Ingenium, so it is all in your hands on your device: see it, export it (Settings, Your data, Backup), delete it (Reset everything). For data an outside service holds, ask that service.

7. Children

Ingenium is not made for children under the age the lawyer sets.
