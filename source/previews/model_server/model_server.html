Drives, flash drives and memory

A drive holds more models; it does not add memory. A model runs in a computer's memory (RAM), so the 2 TB drive lets the Mac mini keep many models and load the one you ask for, while your 8 GB MacBook Pro uses them from the Mac mini instead of running them itself. Flash drives are slow to load from: they are best for carrying models from one computer to another. The Fire tablet's SD card can keep its own small models.

Nothing here goes online: your devices find each other on your home Wi-Fi, sealed. The models named here are examples.
