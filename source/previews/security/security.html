What no lock can do

  • Someone who knows your password, or holds your recovery words, can open everything: keep them apart from the device.
  • A computer already taken over by a harmful program can read what you see on the screen while you work.
  • Code shipped to other people can always be read by someone determined; scrambling makes it slow, signing makes changes show.
  • Lose the password, the words and every other way back in, and the sealed data cannot be opened by anyone, me included.
