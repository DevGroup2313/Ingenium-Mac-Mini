# Build's diamond, Focus's stepping stones and Fabrica's jewelled tree (live preview, 13 November 2026)

`heads2.html` is the page published privately for the owner (https://claude.ai/artifact/Jbyi5rLyb5oZru1eYduGxe, version 3). It is made by
`make.py` from `page.html` (the header cards, the app's own tokens), `hs.js` (Home's click), `diamond.js` (Build: a round brilliant traced
on the graphics chip, its ray of light and fans, the violet aurora, the turned plinth, sparkles), `stones.js` (Focus: stones on rippling
water, and the sound of a stream or soft rain, each rendered once as a seamless loop), `vclight.js` (the compass's light) and `tree.js`
(Fabrica: the tree of r294 in silver and violet glass, with a twisted silver wire, bead settings and diamonds rendered by the same stone).
`make.py` reads the fonts from the scratch kit it was written in: point `S` at a folder holding `cmp13/fonts.json` to build it again.
Nothing here is in any build: these wait for the owner's verdict. The app the tree shows is invented.
