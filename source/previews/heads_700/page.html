Build: the diamond, drawn as a real stone

  • A true round brilliant, traced by the graphics chip: each ray that meets it reflects, refracts in, bounces inside up to six times and leaves split into its colours, so it flashes and throws fire as a cut stone does. It turns once in 36 seconds and rises and settles on Home's seven seconds.
  • A ray of white light enters its crown and leaves as a fan: across the room, back to the left, and down onto the plinth, where it lays a small spectrum. Press the stone and the light runs through it brighter. Light: violets only keeps the fan in Ingenium's violets.
  • Behind it, an aurora in violet and purple only, drawn into the stone's own reflections too; its sparkles are Ingenium's four-pointed star, where a facet sends the light to your eye. The plinth is turned silver, as the compass case is: fine turnings, an engraved scale of 72 cuts, a milled edge.
  • Sound: a press plays Home's own click and nothing else. It draws only while it is in view and the window is shown, at thirty frames a second when still, and at a lower resolution on a slower device.

The stone in the light is your step; the darker ones behind are done. Press one and the water answers.

Focus: stones on water, after your photographs

  • Smooth river stones cross a still pool toward the far bank, each half in the water: wet and dark where the water laps it, matte where it is dry, and mirrored in the water with the sky. No numerals.
  • The water moves as a pool does: a slow swell, small rings lapping round every stone, and every seven seconds a ring spreading from your step. The far water fades into mist and soft points of light.
  • The step in hand catches the light; the steps behind you are darker, washed with violet; the steps ahead are plain slate. Point at a stone and it lifts; press it and it dips, a ring spreads, and you hear a drop.
  • A stream: water running over stones, made of many small bubbles over a soft, low body of water, in a little echo; Soft rain: single drops on leaves and water. Both are made in the app (nothing is fetched), each rendered once as a seamless loop, so they cost almost nothing to play. They stop when Focus is out of view. Fabrica: your tree, in the compass's silver and glass

  • The same tree as now: main is the trunk, each version a bead, an idea brought in an arch back to the trunk, an idea still open a branch, each release a collar with its name, the change not kept yet a bead waiting above the newest. It turns once in 52 seconds, stills under the pointer and turns under a drag.
  • Drawn again in the light that paints Vocatio's compass: the trunk and every branch polished silver, shaded across each rod; each version a bead of violet glass with its window, the light gathered inside it and a pearl rim (an idea brought in is pearl glass, an idea still open a lighter violet); the newest larger, with Ingenium's star inlaid; each release a silver collar.
  • The plinth is turned silver with a violet enamel band, an engraved scale of 72 cuts and the app's name, the foot of the trunk turned to match. Beads, foot and plinth are painted once and reused, so it costs no more than the tree it replaces.
