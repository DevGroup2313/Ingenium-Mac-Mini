2 July 2026. Poster day.

The department's poster day was held in the atrium from ten until one. Priya Venkataraman presented her dissertation poster on spore dispersal in beech litter to the department and won second prize.

Marcus will order the Czapek agar and the pipette tips for next month, and asked everyone to put requests in the sheet by Friday. The cold room in G.14 will be defrosted over the weekend, so nothing should be left on the floor shelves. Training on the new plate reader is arranged for the first week of the month; Hana will run it.

The forest soil was sieved through a 2 mm mesh, homogenised, and stored at 4 °C until the extraction. Colony counts were entered into the shared sheet the same day, with the plate identifiers written as on the lids. Negative controls were run with every batch; none showed growth, and the blanks read within the usual range.
