# Spore germination assay

Version 4, March 2026. Owner: Dr. Imani Okoye. Applies to all Trichoderma and Penicillium strains held in the collection.

## Purpose

To measure the proportion of spores that germinate within a fixed time on a defined medium, so that strains and batches can be compared. Negative controls were run with every batch; none showed growth, and the blanks read within the usual range. Imani repeated the assay on a second day to check that the result did not depend on the batch of medium. The leaf litter came from three sites, and each site was sampled at 7 points along a transect.

## Materials

- Potato dextrose agar plates, poured in G.10 and dried for 30 minutes in the cabinet
- Sterile water with 0.05% Tween 80
- Haemocytometer and a phase-contrast microscope
- Spore suspension adjusted to 1 x 10^6 spores per mL

## Procedure

1. Label six plates per strain with the strain number, the date and your initials.
2. Spread 100 µL of the suspension on each plate with a sterile spreader and let the surface dry.
3. Seal the plates with film and place them lid down in the incubator.
4. Incubate the plates at 24.5 °C for 72 h in the dark. Do not open the incubator door during the first 24 hours.
5. After the incubation, count 100 spores in each of three fields per plate and record germinated and ungerminated spores separately.

### Counting

A spore counts as germinated when the germ tube is at least as long as the spore is wide. The forest soil came from three sites, and each site was sampled at 6 points along a transect. Samples were thawed on ice, vortexed for 30 s, and spun at 7690 g for 14 minutes. The leaf litter was sieved through a 2 mm mesh, homogenised, and stored at 4 °C until the extraction.

### Recording

Enter the counts in the shared sheet the same day. Visitors to G.14 must sign the book and be accompanied at all times. Priya reminded the group that waste from G.16 goes into the yellow bins and sharps into the rigid containers.

## Troubleshooting

- If the control plates show contamination, discard the batch and repeat with fresh medium.
- If germination is under 40% on the reference strain, check the age of the stock before anything else.
