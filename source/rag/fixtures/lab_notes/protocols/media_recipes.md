# Media recipes

Quantities are for 1 litre. Autoclave according to the liquid cycle.

## Potato dextrose agar (PDA)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Potato infusion | 200 g |
| Dextrose | 20 g |
| Agar | 15 g |

## Malt extract agar (MEA)

```
malt extract   20 g
peptone         1 g
glucose        20 g
agar           20 g
```

## Notes

Cultures were inspected daily under the dissecting microscope, and any plate with a foreign colony was discarded. Sven repeated the assay on a second day to check that the result did not depend on the batch of medium. Priya prepared 9 replicates of potato dextrose agar and logged the lot numbers in the notebook. All media were autoclaved and poured in G.12, and the plates were dried for 42 minutes in the cabinet.

Check the pH after autoclaving and adjust only with sterile acid or base.
