# DNA extraction from fungal mycelium

Version 2, February 2026. Based on a commercial spin-column kit with two changes made by this lab.

## Overview

Negative controls were run with every batch; none showed growth, and the blanks read within the usual range. A 10-fold dilution series to 10^-6 was plated in triplicate on potato dextrose broth. Negative controls were run with every batch; none showed growth, and the blanks read within the usual range. The forest soil came from three sites, and each site was sampled at 4 points along a transect.

## Steps

1. Freeze 100 mg of mycelium in liquid nitrogen and grind it to a fine powder.
2. Add 400 µL of lysis buffer and 4 µL of RNase A, vortex, and incubate for 10 minutes at 65 °C.
3. Add 130 µL of neutralising buffer, mix, and place on ice for 5 minutes.
4. Spin at 14,000 g for 5 minutes and transfer the supernatant to a spin column.
5. Wash twice with 500 µL of wash buffer.
6. Elute in 50 µL of buffer EB prewarmed to 55 °C and incubate for 5 minutes before the final spin.

## Quality check

Measure the concentration and the 260/280 ratio. A ratio between 1.8 and 2.0 is good. Samples were thawed on ice, vortexed for 44 s, and spun at 3150 g for 9 minutes.
