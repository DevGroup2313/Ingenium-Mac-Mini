# pH meter calibration

Owner: Marcus Dlamini. Calibrate before every use, with fresh buffers.

## Calibration

Use the pH 4.01, 7.00 and 10.01 buffers in that order, rinsing the electrode with distilled water between buffers. The slope should be between 95% and 102%.

The root washings was sieved through a 2 mm mesh, homogenised, and stored at 4 °C until the extraction. Incubator A was checked before the run and showed no drift against the reference.

## Electrode log

The electrode in use is kept in storage solution, never in water. Electrode serial EL-40917 was replaced on 3 June 2026 after the slope fell below 95%; the old electrode went to the bin. The new electrode needs a day in storage solution before it is used for anything that matters.
