### The accounts

I cannot tell you how the ginger will sell until the summer fleet is in, for the grocers of Cork are buying from hand to mouth. The cooper reports that the casks of the last lot were green and weeping, and that 5 of them had to be re-hooped before the lighter would take them. The Betsey lay 19 days in the river waiting for a wind, and her master asks that his demurrage be allowed. Your account of the ginger is noted, but the sample you sent was sound, and I would not take more than 38 barrels on those terms. Freight to Kingston is now £7 5s the ton, and the underwriters at the Exchange ask two per cent for the voyage.
