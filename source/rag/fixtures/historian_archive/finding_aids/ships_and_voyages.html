Voyages

The mate mustered the people at four bells and found every man present, which had not happened since leaving Savanna-la-Mar. Served the people their allowance of beef and pork, with a pint of rum between four, as is the custom of the voyage. The pumps were worked an hour in every watch, the ship being somewhat leaky in the upper works.

  1. Captain Pryce thought the Mary Ann sailed best with a little weather helm and her topsails reefed.
  2. Spoke a brig bound for Falmouth, whose master told us that mahogany was dear there and the roads full of shipping.
  3. It is the opinion of the underwriters that the risk to Savanna-la-Mar is not increased by the late news, and I have insured accordingly.
