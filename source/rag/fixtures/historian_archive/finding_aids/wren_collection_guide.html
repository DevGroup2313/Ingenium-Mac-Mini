The Wren Papers: a guide to the collection

Scope and content

The papers of the Bristol sugar house of Wren & Hollis, 1779–1802. The collection comprises 214 letters, 9 account books and 31 loose maps, deposited at Bristol Archives by Eleanor Marsh in 1962.

Seen from the counting house, the abolition debates of the period were at first a distant noise, and only later a matter of direct concern. The cooper's trade was as important as the shipwright's, for a cask that leaked was a cargo lost, and the cost fell on the owner. Freight rates moved with the season and with the news from Europe, and a rumour of war could double the cost of a voyage within a week.

Access

Reading room

The papers may be consulted in the reading room by appointment. Some volumes are fragile and are issued only in the presence of a member of staff.

Copying

Photography is allowed without flash. Copies of individual items can be ordered, and the archive asks that users acknowledge the collection as Bristol Archives, Wren Papers.
