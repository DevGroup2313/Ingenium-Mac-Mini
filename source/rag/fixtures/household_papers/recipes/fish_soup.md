# Fish soup

## Ingredients

- 600 g white fish
- 1 leek
- 2 potatoes
- 1 litre of stock
- dill

## Method

Sweat the leek, add the potatoes and stock, and simmer for fifteen minutes. Add the fish and cook until it flakes. The postman left a card on Sunday; the parcel is at the sorting office until the end of the week.
