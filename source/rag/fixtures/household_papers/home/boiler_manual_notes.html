Boiler notes

Pressure

With the heating off and the system cold the pressure gauge should read between 1.0 and 1.5 bar. If it falls below 1.0, top it up with the filling loop and close the loop again.

Service

The boiler needs a service every October. The window cleaner came on Wednesday and said the bathroom would need another visit, which he will confirm by message. The postman left a card on Saturday; the parcel is at the sorting office until the end of the week. Lars has put the bins out on Wednesday; the recycling goes out on alternate weeks.
