# Recipes (`source/recipes/`)

How Ingenium's designs are made, written as recipes the owner can bring into Ingenium's Folders and follow again for other apps.
Her words (14 November 2026): "I want for the way that the pages animated popped out 3D headers were designed and constructed to be saved
into my Folders in Ingenium and preserved so that it is easier to replicate the process later"; asked where they should live, she chose
to have them built into the app.

- `Ingenium/3D header recipe/`: 18 notes. The first, "00 How I build a 3D header", is the recipe; the other "00" notes give its parts
  (the card, metal and colour, motion, drawing methods, speed, her rules, from preview to release); one note for each page's header.
- The folder layout is the build library's own (`modules/r258.js`): the top folder names the app, the next one the group, and each
  `.md` file is one note. Zipped as it stands (the `Ingenium` folder at the top of the zip), it can be brought into Folders with
  "Bring in more notes"; from 7.03 that adds the notes to the library and removes nothing.
