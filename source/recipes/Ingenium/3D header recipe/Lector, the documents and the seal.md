# Lector, the documents and the seal

Module: modules/r290.js (Ingenium 6.93; the wax seal in 6.94; the sealed dossier in 6.96; the seal kept on the paper in 6.99).

## My words that shaped it

"I don't want pressing the engine button on the Lector page to do anything. I just want to use it as a fidget button on the page for my amusement, I like the clicking and the animation that goes with it matching that of the one from the Home page"; "redraw the seal as a wax seal pressed into the paper, in the ribbon's violet and the paper's texture, while keeping the clicks and flourishes you like from Home"; "Please remove the bow, I hate it and it cheapens the look of the design"; "there is a clear bug that keeps on disappearing the wax seal. I hate that."

## What it is

The documents of a reading as a fan of five sheets of grained paper, each with its tab, its initial, its title, a few lines and its numeral, two loose sheets behind; a band round the dossier in front with the wax seal at its heart and the ribbon's two tails under it. The seal is Ingenium's engine logo pressed in wax, with INGENIUM round the top and the day's date in Latin round the foot: white wax by day, the engine button's night by night.

## How it is made

- The sheets are drawn on canvases under the seal's light, each at its own depth, so they slide past one another as the fan sways as one piece, on Home's engine button's 7 seconds.
- No CSS filter inside the 3D frame: the sheets' shadows are painted as part of each sheet. Safari's engine had drawn such a filter in a layer of its own and set it down out of place (a dark patch beside the seal on the Mac).
- The seal casts no shadow of its own (a near black dot behind it in 6.95), and it stands 6 pixels off the paper in every state, so a press never sends it behind the paper.

## How it answers

The fan leans toward the pointer; the seal sways, leans, lifts, sinks and plays Home's flourishes, and does nothing else: it is a fidget button.
