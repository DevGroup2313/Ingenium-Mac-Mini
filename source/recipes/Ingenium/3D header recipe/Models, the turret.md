# Models, the turret

Module: modules/r269.js (Ingenium 6.77, 11 November 2026); the rooms below it in modules/r267.js.

"The Models page needs a redesign and a major makeover to match the rest of the Ingenium's pages, it looks a little basic compared to the rest of the app." On the previews: "I want Ingenium's colours preserved with the new designs", "I like the turret version but the colours are off", and "I choose the Turret, please push it everywhere." Then: "I want your turret to replace the other chat's turret design instead. I preferred your colours and designs better."

## What it is

A turret of lenses, as a microscope's, in Ingenium's lavender and violet (no gold). Each model on this device is a lens set in the turret, its glass in the model's own colour and its name beneath it; five stand in the turret at most (the one in the engine always among them); one port is empty. The scale round the turret is this device's memory for a model; the card beside it says what is in the engine.

## How it answers

Pressing a lens turns the turret until that lens stands under the engine's mark at the top, and loads the model; pressing it again, or the empty port, unloads it. A model too heavy for this device asks first, and a busy engine is never interrupted. Every lens lifts under the pointer and sinks when pressed; the turn ticks softly, once for each port it passes. By night the metal, the glass and the scale take the night's colours. Below 1060 pixels the turret stands under the words; below 760 it is scaled to the width.
