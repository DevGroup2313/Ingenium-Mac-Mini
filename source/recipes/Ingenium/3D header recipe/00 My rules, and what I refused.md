# My rules for headers, and what I refused

## My rules

- A live interactive preview before any new look or anything new; only what I approve is shipped.
- Measure the app's own surfaces first; never invent a new look beside them.
- Every key lifts, sinks and sounds; every surface raised, never flat, and never heavier for its own sake.
- No gold in Ingenium. No purple glow, gloss or gradients on keys beyond the app's own key surfaces and its filled key.
- Never tilt an instrument away from me: it always faces me.
- Ingenium's own sounds only (Home's click), unless I ask for another sound.
- Gentle motion, never brusque.
- Each app's documents in its own palette and seal; never one app's colours on another's.
- When I ask for one thing, change only that thing. Leave the rest alone unless I ask (14 November 2026: "I don't want you to remove anything from Vocatio's compass, please leave it alone since I never asked for that").

## What I refused, and why

These are kept so that no new header repeats them.

- **Thicker, heavier surfaces.** "I already hate this redesign, you're just making everything thicker and ugly." (Home, 10 November 2026)
- **Variations of one idea.** "I don't like any of them, they all look basic and variations of the same design. This feels lazy and unoriginal. Please do it again and go deeper." (Home, 10 November 2026)
- **An object leaning away from me.** "This looks worse and it is not even facing me, its slanted at a weird angle. I HATE IT" (Blend, 10 November 2026)
- **Parts that look pasted on.** The engine logo "does not look built in, it looks placed on top badly" (Blend, 10 November 2026); the first lenses tried in Vocatio's dial looked "fake and pasted on the page" (as kept in modules/r290.js).
- **A flat engine.** "I also don't want Ingenium's Engine to look flat either" (Home, 10 November 2026).
- **Glitter for its own sake.** Fabrica's tree: "it looks fake and just sparkly and glittery and not at all refined, I hate it, please remove the shimmer effect and make the animation and design more refined and elegant" (13 November 2026). Even on the diamond, sparkle must stay refined: see "Too much" below.
- **Something I did not ask for.** "No I never asked for raised balls, I specifically said that I want the ingenium orrery design from the compass, what is this???" (Build, 14 November 2026)
- **Brusque motion.** "they are too brusque and intense, they are fast and curt and detract from the smooth visuals of the page" (14 November 2026)
- **Sounds remade worse.** "The stones water sounds sound horrible please redo", then "please revert it back to the previous water sounds" (Focus, 14 November 2026): the approved sounds came back.
- **A circle and a ripple on a press.** The circle round the diamond and the ripple across the holder's Orrery (14 November 2026): removed in 7.03, the press itself kept.
- **The full spectrum on Build.** "please revert the full spectrum northern lights and only leave them in the Focus page, I think the violet hues look better on the Build page without the full spectrum" (14 November 2026). Each page keeps its own palette.
- **Too much.** More sparkles, glittering points, starburst rays and a shimmer on the diamond, tried one after another on 14 November 2026: "Please remove the shimmers from the diamond, too much will cheapen the look of the diamond"; "The sparkles and shimmers on the diamond is too much, I already told you to reduce and remove them"; "The whole thing looks horrible, revert it back". When I ask for more, add a little, show it, and stop; when I say it is too much, go back to what I approved.
- **Charcoal on a violet design.** The engine logo's charcoal face by night on Build's holder became deep indigo (7.04).
