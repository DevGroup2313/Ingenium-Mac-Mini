# Folders, the drawer

Module: modules/r263.js (Ingenium 6.69, 10 November 2026); the drawer's pulls in modules/r279.js (6.85).

## My words that shaped it

"The Folders page needs its own design and not just be a replica of the Blend page and Home page." "Also, I want the apps icon logos on their folders please." On the preview: "I like this new look, please fix the stretched icons and push it everywhere." And: "I want the apps seals to switch to their dark mode look when the app is on Dark mode".

## What it is

A filing system of its own, in Ingenium's colours, type and depth, with no orbit and no sky: a drawer pulled open toward me, with a folder for each app standing in it, each with its tab, its papers and its app's own seal (IngSeal, modules/r140.js, by day or by night as the Display is). The front folder is the one used last.

## How it is made

- Every folder is an index card, ruled and punched, with its seal; the build library is a bound volume on its shelf; the ways of bringing work in are envelopes in a tray; How I work is a ruled sheet with Ingenium's seal pressed faintly in its corner.
- A folder opened is a spread: the inside of its cover on the left (its seal, its name, where I left off, how I work on it), its papers on the right (how it was built, the decisions, its conversations, its repository, its words).
- The drawer's two pulls are keys in its own grooves: the left finds a folder as I type, the right orders the folders (used last, A to Z, Z to A, newest, oldest).

## How it answers

Pointing at a folder lifts it out of the drawer and names it; pressing it opens it. Every card, envelope, row and note leans toward the pointer, lifts under it and sinks when pressed, and sounds. Left alone, nothing is rewritten.
