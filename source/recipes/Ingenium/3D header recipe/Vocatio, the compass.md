# Vocatio, the compass

Modules: modules/r290.js (the heads and the shared kit, Ingenium 6.93, 12 November 2026) and modules/r293.js (which turns the compass on, 6.94 and 6.95); the medallions in 6.98.

## My words that shaped it

"I want regal and elevated to match these pages ... refined, elevated and elegant while being 3D ... theme related without being on the nose ... like they are built-in". Of the medallions: "I want the balls inside of the compass on the Career Studio to be refined and elevated to match the texture and look of the compass but I still want to be animated match the page and the rest of Ingenium", and "I want the size of the little balls inside of Vocatio's compass to be remain smaller in their original sizes, they look really odd bigger".

## What it is

A vintage silver pocket compass after my photographs, in Ingenium's silver, pearl and violet, with Home's own engine button at its heart and Ingenium's Orrery engraved on its dial. The counsellor's stages are medallions set into the bezel, each with its numeral.

## How it is made

- The light model (vcEnv, vcSilver, vcEnamel) lights every part as polished silver and enamel in a photographer's room; parts are painted once and kept as pictures.
- Each medallion is painted pixel by pixel through the case's own light (vcMedalUrl): a milled silver edge of 44 ribs, a polished bevel, a sunken groove, and a domed face of enamel over an engine turned pattern, its numeral in Cormorant, raised in silver on violet or inlaid in violet on pearl, at its original size.
- The compass's 3D layers hide the medallions from the pointer, so the compass finds the medallion under the pointer from its own geometry.
- The compass hands its Orrery (its enamel dial and its engraving) to Build's holder through IngStudios.kit.dial, so both show the same Orrery.

## How it answers

The compass sways on a 10 second clock and leans toward the pointer on a spring. A press on the ring spins it, the rim knocks, the bow sways, a medallion sinks and opens its stage, and the engine button at the heart plays Home's flourishes. Each press sounds. I asked for the compass to be left exactly as it is (14 November 2026).
