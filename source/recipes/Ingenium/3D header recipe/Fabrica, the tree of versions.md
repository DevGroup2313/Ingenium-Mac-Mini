# Fabrica, the tree of versions

Module: modules/r294.js (Ingenium 6.96, 13 November 2026; for every app, pressable and without its glitch in 7.01).

## My words that shaped it

"I also want the Fabrica page to get the same makeover as the others ... I think that it is missing its header card with the nice 3D animation"; "For Fabrica, I want the tree design but I want it refined and elevated to match the Vocatio’s compass, and the other designs inside of Ingenium"; "it looks fake and just sparkly and glittery and not at all refined, I hate it, please remove the shimmer effect"; "remove the glitch from Fabrica's tree, I think its a bug"; "I want the balls on Fabrica's tree to be animated and clickable and have the same effect as the engine button from the Home page"; "they have no bounce back when I click on them".

## What it is

The open app's versions as a tree turning slowly on a plinth of spun metal: main is the trunk; each version a bead of violet glass along it, the newest at the top with Ingenium's engine star; each idea a branch leaving the trunk where it parted from main; an idea brought in an arch back to the trunk; each release a collar round the trunk; changes not kept yet a bead not yet set above the newest. It stands for every app, even one with a single version.

## How it is made

- Drawn on a canvas by projecting every part in 3D as the tree turns, lit with the compass's light model, at its true size so its fine lines do not shimmer.
- Every part is seen from the same place, a little above. Before 7.01 the branches were placed as if seen from below while the plinth was seen from above, so they swung up and down as the tree turned (the highest point travelled 112 pixels; now 11).
- Glass beads in fine silver, each painted once for its size and kind and then only placed, so nothing jumps as the tree turns; no shimmer, as I asked.

## How it answers

Pointing at the tree stills it; dragging turns it. A version lifts under the pointer and says its words. Each ball beats softly at rest with the engine button's heartbeat, out of step with the others; pressed, it sinks while held, springs back when let go and plays one of the engine button's flourishes with its glow and ripple. The engine star at the top turns as the engine logo does and spins when pressed; the trunk and plinth turn the tree. Reading only: nothing in the app is written by the tree.
