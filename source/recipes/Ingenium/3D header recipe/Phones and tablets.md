# Phones and tablets

Modules: modules/r296.js (the studios' headers on a phone, Ingenium 7.00) and modules/r261.js (Home on a phone, from 6.71).

## My words

"I also want the new pages designs and graphics visible on the Iphone/Ipad and Fire Tablet screens too, last time the Home page sphere’s design was not on the iphone’s update, I don’t want that happening again, please make sure that it doesn’t and that my app looks updated and refined on all screens and does not revert to its old designs ever again." (13 November 2026)

## How it is made

- Below 760 pixels a studio's instrument stands under its words, drawn at its own size (520 by 424) and scaled down whole, so it looks as it does on a computer: Vocatio's compass, Lector's documents, Fabrica's tree and Build's diamond. Focus's pool takes the card's width by itself.
- A tablet keeps the computer's layout, scaled.
- Home's orrery stacks under the greeting on a phone, its orbit tilted 44 degrees.
- The iPhone and iPad app serves the page that ships inside it, so each update carries the new designs; nothing in the app keeps an older page.

## How to check it

Open the release page at the sizes of an iPhone, an iPad and a Fire tablet (both ways) and look at every page. On 14 November 2026 (7.02) every redesigned page showed its new design at those sizes in Chromium. Safari's own engine, which iPhones and iPads use, could not be run on the test machine, so the last check is on my own devices.
