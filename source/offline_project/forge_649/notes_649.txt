The seal in About is a key, as Carpe Diem's is: Tab reaches it, it sinks while pressed and knocks when let go (Enter and
Space too), and it keeps still when movement is reduced. Tabula, Abacus and Imago inside the Forge wear the Forge's seal,
name and colour in their Seals & letterheads group, their watermark and their signed pages, and their Word files name no
other app. The Windows app opens a lighter page (14 MB, not 72 MB): the apps the Forge carries stay in files beside it,
read the first time they are wanted, and your work stays where it was. The Windows app has its own identity on the taskbar
and in the Start menu.
